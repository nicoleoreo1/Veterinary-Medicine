Veterinary Notes

Causes of Fever

Infectious

  • Bacterial infection (e.g., pyometra, abscess, pneumonia)
  • Viral infection (e.g., distemper, parvovirus, FIP)
  • Fungal infection (e.g., histoplasmosis, blastomycosis)
  • Rickettsial disease (e.g., Rocky Mountain spotted fever, ehrlichiosis)
  • Protozoal infection (e.g., toxoplasmosis, babesiosis)
  • Leptospirosis
  • Tick-borne diseases

Immune-Mediated

  • Immune-mediated hemolytic anemia (IMHA)
  • Immune-mediated thrombocytopenia (ITP)
  • Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Polyarthritis
  • Vasculitis

Neoplasia

  • Lymphoma
  • Leukemia
  • Solid tumors with necrosis
  • Paraneoplastic syndrome

Inflammatory / Other

  • Pancreatitis
  • Polyarthritis (non-infectious)
  • Hepatitis
  • Drug reaction / drug fever
  • Vaccine reaction
  • Heat stroke
  • Hyperthyroidism (cats)
  • Fever of unknown origin (FUO)

Tissue Damage

  • Trauma
  • Surgery / post-operative fever
  • Thromboembolism
  • Infarction
